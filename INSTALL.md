# Setting up your own Pantry Cache

A list of what is in your freezers, fridges, pantry — anywhere you keep things —
that you can look at from a tablet or a phone. It keeps everything in a Google
spreadsheet that belongs to you.

Setting it up takes about ten minutes. You do not need to know anything about
code, and there is nothing to install on any device.

---

## First: check the signal where you will use it

**Do this before anything else.** It takes two minutes and it decides whether
the rest is worth doing.

Walk to every place you want to keep track of — the garage, the shed, the porch,
the cellar — and open any web page on the tablet or phone you plan to use, while
standing where you would actually stand.

**If there is no signal somewhere, this app cannot be used there.** Not slowly:
it will not open at all. That is how it works, and no future version will change
it. A paper list on the door is a better tool for a freezer out of range.

If every spot has a signal, carry on.

---

## 1. Make your own copy

Open the link you were sent and choose **Make a copy**.

That is the whole of this step. A spreadsheet appears in your own Google Drive
with the app already inside it. It is yours: nobody else can see it, and nothing
you do can affect anyone else's.

---

## 2. Switch it on

In your new spreadsheet, wait a few seconds for a **Pantry Cache** menu to
appear along the top, next to *Help*. Then choose:

**Pantry Cache → Open the app / get my link**

A window opens and walks you through the last part, which is mostly clicking
**Deploy**. Follow it to the end.

Along the way Google will ask whether you trust this app, and will warn you that
it is "unverified". That is expected. It is your own copy asking for permission
to use your own spreadsheet — there is nobody else involved. Choose **Advanced**,
then **Go to Pantry Cache (unsafe)**, then **Allow**.

When you are done, open **Pantry Cache → Open the app / get my link** once more.
It now shows you a web address. That address is your app.

---

## 3. Put it on the tablet

Email the address to yourself, open it on the tablet, and use the browser's
**Add to Home screen**. It then opens like any other app, full screen.

---

## 4. Tell it where you keep things

The first time it opens, it asks. Give each place a name — *Garage*, *Pantry*,
*Shed* — say where it is if that helps, and pick a colour so you can tell them
apart at a glance.

You can add more, rename them or change their colours later, from the gear in
the top corner. Nothing here has to be right first time.

Then start putting things in.

---

## What is yours, and what to know

**The spreadsheet is yours.** It lives in your Google account. Whoever sent you
the link cannot see it, cannot change it, and cannot get it back for you. Open
it any time — the app is only a convenient way to read and write it, and every
number is there in plain columns.

**Your web address is a key.** Anyone who has it can open your list and change
it, without signing in. That is what makes it painless on a tablet by the
freezer, and for a list of frozen raspberries it is a fair trade. The part worth
knowing in advance: **you cannot take the address back**. Shutting it off means
making a new one, which then has to be re-added to the tablet. So treat it a
little carefully — it is the kind of thing that ends up forwarded in an email
rather than handed out on purpose.

If that ever stops feeling comfortable, the deploy settings have *Who has
access* → **Anyone with a Google account**, which costs one sign-in on the
tablet and nothing else.

**Your backups are yours.** Google keeps a history of the sheet under
**File → Version history**, which undoes most accidents. For a copy you keep
yourself, **File → Download → Microsoft Excel**.

**It is given as it is.** Nobody is running a service behind this. If the tablet
breaks or a tab gets deleted, that is yours to sort out — which is the same
reason nothing anyone else does can ever affect your copy.

---

## Keeping it up to date

Your copy does not change on its own, which is deliberate: nothing anyone
publishes can alter your list without you asking for it.

When you want to check, choose **Pantry Cache → Check for updates**. If there is
a newer version it tells you what changed and hands you each file to paste, in
order, with a button to copy each one. Your data is never touched by this.
